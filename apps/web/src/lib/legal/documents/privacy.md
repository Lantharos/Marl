This policy explains what personal information Marl collects, why, and the choices you have. Marl is operated by Lantharos, which is the controller of the personal information described here. You can reach us at privacy@marl.sh.

## What we collect

**Account information.** Your name, username, email addresses, profile picture, biography, and website. Your password is stored only as a salted hash. Passkeys are stored as public keys, and two-factor secrets are encrypted.

**Content.** Repositories, commits, issues, pulls, reviews, comments, releases, and files you upload. Commit metadata such as author names and email addresses is part of the Git history you push.

**Security and session data.** When you sign in we record the IP address and browser for each session so you can review and end sessions, and so we can detect abuse. Sensitive actions are written to an audit log with the account that performed them.

**Service data.** Operational logs about requests, errors, and performance. These logs are used to keep Marl running and are retained for a limited period.

**Communications.** Messages you send us, reports you file about content, and records of email we send you.

We do not use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or build advertising profiles.

## How we use it

- To provide Marl: host your repositories, show your profile and contributions, deliver notifications, and run the features you use.
- To keep Marl secure: authenticate you, prevent abuse and spam, rate-limit automated traffic, and investigate reports.
- To communicate with you: account verification, security alerts, notifications you have enabled, and notices about changes to our terms.
- To meet legal obligations, such as responding to valid legal requests.

Our legal bases under the GDPR are the performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interest in keeping Marl secure and reliable, your consent where we ask for it, and compliance with legal obligations.

## What others can see

Your username, name, profile picture, biography, website, public repositories, and public contributions are visible to anyone. Activity in private repositories is visible only to people with access to them. Email addresses are never shown on your profile, but an email address you use as a Git commit author is part of that commit and is visible to anyone who can read the repository.

## Service providers

Marl runs on Cloudflare, which hosts our application, database, file storage, and email delivery. Cloudflare processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. If you enable webhooks or import a repository from another service, Marl sends or requests data from the addresses you configure.

Data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Cookies

Marl uses a small number of cookies that are needed for the service to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in, a device cookie that keeps one session per device, and a cookie that remembers your chosen appearance. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

## Retention

We keep account information while your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal information is removed immediately. Content you created in other people's repositories remains attributed to a deleted user so that discussions stay readable. Your own repositories are hidden immediately and permanently deleted after 30 days.

Operational logs are kept for up to 30 days. Database backups used for disaster recovery are kept for up to 30 days, after which deleted information no longer exists in them. Records of content reports and moderation decisions are kept as long as needed to handle appeals and repeated abuse.

## Your rights

You can view and update your profile in account settings, download a copy of your data from **Settings → Data and deletion**, and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Write to privacy@marl.sh for any request we cannot handle in the product; we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

Marl is not intended for children under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, contact privacy@marl.sh and we will remove it.

## Changes

We will notify you by email or in Marl before a material change to this policy takes effect.
