Marl is a place to build software together. This policy describes what is not allowed, so that repositories, discussions, and runners stay safe and useful for everyone. It is part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## Content that is not allowed

- **Malware and exploits used for harm.** Code designed to be deployed against systems without authorisation, including ransomware, credential stealers, botnets, and droppers. Security research, proof-of-concept exploits, and defensive tooling are welcome when they are clearly presented as such and do not target live systems.
- **Unlawful content.** Material that is illegal where it is published, including child sexual abuse material, which we report to the relevant authorities.
- **Infringing content.** Content that infringes copyright, trademarks, or other rights. See the [Copyright policy](/legal/copyright) for how to report it.
- **Private information.** Publishing someone else's personal information, credentials, or confidential data without their permission.
- **Harassment and hate.** Threats, targeted harassment, or content that promotes violence or hatred against people based on who they are.
- **Deception.** Impersonating people or organisations, phishing, and misleading others about the origin of software.
- **Sexually explicit content** outside of repositories where it is clearly necessary for the project's legitimate purpose.

## Behaviour that is not allowed

- **Spam.** Bulk-created accounts or repositories, unsolicited promotion, and automated comments, issues, or pulls that do not contribute to a project.
- **Abusing the service.** Using Marl as general file storage or a content delivery network, running cryptocurrency mining through hosted features, or placing excessive load on the service.
- **Circumventing limits.** Creating accounts or organisations to get around limits, suspensions, or bans.
- **Attacking Marl or its users.** Probing, scanning, or testing the vulnerability of Marl without permission. To report a security issue, write to legal@marl.sh; please give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it.

## Agents and automation

Automated accounts, including agent accounts, must be operated by a person or organization who is responsible for them. They must respect rate limits and must not open issues, pulls, or comments in repositories they have not been invited to, unless that project has asked for them.

## Reporting and enforcement

You can report a repository, issue, pull, comment, or account from its page using **Report**, or by writing to abuse@marl.sh. Reports are reviewed by people. Depending on the severity, we may remove content, disable a repository, restrict features, or suspend an account. Serious or repeated violations lead to permanent suspension.

When we take action, we tell the affected owner what was removed and why, unless doing so would put others at risk or break the law. You can appeal a decision by replying to our notice.
